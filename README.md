# Maneeshwari Akka - Birthday Surprise Website 🎂✨

A personal, heartfelt, and interactive birthday surprise website created by **Dhana** for his elder sister by heart, **Maneeshwari Akka**.

---

## 🌟 Features & Exact Flow

1. **Opening Surprise Page**
   - Portrait photo (`M1`) positioned on the right side.
   - Left-aligned heartfelt message: *"For Maneeshwari"*
   - Clearly visible birthday date: **06 NOVEMBER 2026**
   - Interactive button: **"OPEN YOUR SURPRISE"**

2. **Timed 4-Second Birthday Reveal**
   - Photo (`M2`) beside the wish: **"HAPPY BIRTHDAY MANEESH AKKA"**
   - Celebration animations:
     - Vaana Vedikai (sky rockets shooting up & bursting with star sparks)
     - Busvanam (ground flowerpot fountain crackers)
     - Floating balloons & colorful confetti bursts
   - **Stops automatically after exactly 4 seconds**, keeping the photo and wish visible with the prompt to continue.

3. **Personal Question Screen**
   - Heading: *"Before You See Your Surprise"*
   - Question: *"Who do you like the most?"*
   - Choices:
     - **Future Husband**: Displays playful teasing (*"There’s still plenty of time for that! 😂 Choose the other option for now! ❤️"*) and stays on the screen.
     - **Dhana**: Displays appreciation (*"Awww! ❤️ Thank you so much! Did you choose me because you think I’m a good boy? 😂 Your affection means so much to me, Akka!"*) and unlocks **"GO FOR SURPRISE"**.

4. **Our Story Timeline (15 Detailed Memories)**
   - Heading: **"HOW A COLLEGE SENIOR BECAME MY AKKA"**
   - All 15 memories presented chronologically with warm first-person narrative:
     - January 4, 7:45 PM idly shop meeting
     - "Did you eat?" + photo **`M3`**
     - Guidance, fights, and learning
     - Short scooter rides + photo **`M4`**
     - "Neeyum naanum aalu" & photo teasing
     - Phone call after bike accident
     - College bus conversation on her last day
     - Tears of joy for her offer letter
     - A bond that transcends blood

5. **Cake-Cutting Celebration**
   - Heading: **"MAKE A WISH, MANEESH AKKA!"**
   - Interactive SVG cake with 3 flickering candles.
   - Clicking **"Cut the Birthday Cake 🎂"** animates the knife slicing down, parts the cake halves, puffs candle smoke, shoots confetti, and reveals the birthday wish.

6. **The Little Things That Mean So Much**
   - Dedicated gratitude section honoring everyday care (rose milk, tiffin box, sharing meals, advice).
   - Polaroid gallery featuring **`M5`** (*HOME selfie*) and **`M6`** (*outdoor memory*).

7. **The Special Heartbeat Moment**
   - Playful animation:
     - *"Normally, my heartbeat goes… Lap tap… lap tap…"*
     - Transition to: *"But whenever I talk to you… Maneesh… Maneesh… Maneesh… Maneesh… ❤️"*
     - Animated pulsating heart visualizer.

8. **The Birthday Letter**
   - Complete, unabridged personal letter from Dhana on vintage parchment with a wax seal.
   - Sibling sign-off: *"Your Thambi, Dhana ❤️"*

9. **Personal Audio Section (Immediately After Letter)**
   - Displays exact prompt message:
     *“Some songs hold feelings that words can never fully explain. ❤️ This one has a personal connection, so I wanted you to listen to it as a little piece of my heart, made especially for this surprise. 🎧💗”*
   - Custom player for **`Ethana Thanima Vattum Bothum - Magale _ Baththa _ Tamil.mp3`**:
     - Play / Pause button
     - Real-time seeker & timestamp formatting
     - Equalizer soundwave pulse
     - Replay button
     - (No video player)

10. **The Final Sibling Promise**
    - Step 1: **Best Wishes, By Dhana**
    - Step 2: **`D1`** — Dhana's solo beach photograph.
    - Step 3: Bold final declaration:
      **THROUGH THICK AND THIN, TOGETHER FOREVER. ♾️❤️**

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open PowerShell or Terminal in this folder:
   ```powershell
   cd "c:\Users\dhana\OneDrive\Desktop\bday 2"
   ```

2. Run the development server:
   ```powershell
   npm run dev
   ```

3. Open your browser and go to:
   ```
   http://localhost:5173/
   ```

4. To build for production / hosting:
   ```powershell
   npm run build
   ```
   The production-ready static files will be in the `dist/` directory.
